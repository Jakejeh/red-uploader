Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

What we access: basic TikTok profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) and permission to post videos, only for the account that authorizes the app.

How it is used: solely to upload and publish the account owner's own videos. Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never sold, shared or sent to third parties.

Other people's data: none is collected.

Deletion: the owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Apps and services), and local tokens are deleted on request.
